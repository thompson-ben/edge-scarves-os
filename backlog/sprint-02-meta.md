# Sprint 2 — Meta (Profitable Acquisition)

**Dates:** TODO (2 weeks) · **Status:** ⚪ Planned · **Phase:** 2 (Acquisition)

## Objective

Stand up a disciplined, profitable Meta acquisition engine: verified tracking, a clean prospecting→retargeting structure, a first batch of tested creative, and spend governed by break-even ROAS. By the end we should know our break-even ROAS and have at least one profitable (or clearly diagnosed) campaign.

## Business Justification

Once the store converts (Sprint 1), profitable paid traffic is the fastest way to scale toward £2,000/month net. But spend only adds profit above break-even ROAS — so this sprint establishes the economics and tracking first, then tests, before scaling.

Profitable incremental orders and a scalable acquisition channel — provided ROAS ≥ break-even. If below, this sprint prevents loss by diagnosing before scaling.
