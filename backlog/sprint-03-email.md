# Sprint 3 — Email (Lifecycle & Retention)

**Dates:** TODO (2 weeks) · **Status:** ⚪ Planned · **Phase:** 3 (Retention)

## Objective

Launch the core email lifecycle: confirm the ESP, build the highest-intent automated flows (welcome, abandoned cart/checkout, post-purchase), and start a premium-appropriate list-capture mechanism. By the end, email should recover lost sales and begin lifting repeat purchase — at near-zero marginal cost.

## Business Justification

Email is the highest-margin channel: it monetises an audience we already own and increases customer lifetime value. Recovering abandoned carts/checkouts and improving repeat purchase directly lift net profit without extra acquisition spend — essential to a durable path to £2,000/month and beyond.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] ESP confirmed and Shopify integration verified ([`docs/09-email-marketing.md`](../docs/09-email-marketing.md)).
- [ ] Baseline recorded: list size, email % of revenue, existing flow performance.
- [ ] Core flows live: welcome, abandoned cart, abandoned checkout, post-purchase.
- [ ] Premium (non-discount-led) capture mechanism live.
- [ ] First flow performance reported; recovered revenue measured.

Recovered abandoned-cart/checkout revenue plus higher repeat purchase and LTV → higher net profit at near-zero marginal cost.
