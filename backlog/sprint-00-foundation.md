Build the Edge Scarves operating system: a complete, professional repository that is the single source of truth for the business — documentation, AI specialist team, sprint methodology, development standards, KPI definitions and roadmap. **No changes to the Shopify store.**

This sprint is the cheapest, highest-leverage investment: it makes every future sprint faster, safer and accountable to net profit. We document the company before we "hire the engineering team."
