# Research — Seasonal Campaigns

## Why this matters
Scarves are highly seasonal; a planned demand calendar lets us prepare inventory, creative, email and spend ahead of peaks instead of reacting late. Feeds [`docs/05-product-strategy.md`](../../docs/05-product-strategy.md), [`docs/08-meta-ads.md`](../../docs/08-meta-ads.md), [`docs/09-email-marketing.md`](../../docs/09-email-marketing.md) and the [roadmap](../../docs/16-roadmap.md).

## What to capture
- **Seasonal demand calendar:** peaks/troughs by month (autumn/winter, Christmas, Mother's Day, Valentine's, etc.)
- Per key moment: lead times, inventory needs, creative/email angles, budget shifts
- Post-mortems of past campaigns (what worked / didn't) — once we have them
- Cut-off dates for gift delivery and how to communicate them
- **So what:** a repeatable seasonal playbook per moment

## Key questions
- What is our true demand curve across the year?
- How far ahead must we prepare stock and creative for each peak?
- Which one or two seasonal moments matter most to profit?

> Build the calendar from our real sales seasonality where possible, supplemented by category research (cite sources).
