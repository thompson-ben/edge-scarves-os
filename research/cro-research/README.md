# Research — CRO Research

## Why this matters
A library of conversion principles, test ideas and case studies feeds a stronger, evidence-based hypothesis backlog — so we test things likely to work rather than guessing. Feeds [`docs/07-conversion-rate-optimisation.md`](../../docs/07-conversion-rate-optimisation.md) and [`docs/06-website-audit.md`](../../docs/06-website-audit.md).

## What to capture
- Conversion principles (clarity, friction reduction, trust, value framing)
- Catalogued test ideas by page (PDP, cart, checkout, homepage) → hypothesis backlog
- Case studies / experiment write-ups (with results & source)
- Testing methodology notes (significance, sample size, low-traffic approaches)
- **So what:** specific hypotheses to add, ICE-triaged then scored for the backlog

## Key questions
- What conversion changes have the strongest evidence for stores like ours?
- How do we test validly given our traffic volume?
- Which ideas address our specific audit findings and customer objections?

> Adapt evidence to our context and premium brand. Cite case-study sources; never present a borrowed result as our own.
