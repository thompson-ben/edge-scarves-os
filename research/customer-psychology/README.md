# Research — Customer Psychology

## Why this matters
Conversion and marketing improve fastest when we understand *why* people buy — the motivations, emotions, objections and decision triggers behind a premium scarf or gift purchase. Feeds [`docs/03-customer-persona.md`](../../docs/03-customer-persona.md), [`docs/07-conversion-rate-optimisation.md`](../../docs/07-conversion-rate-optimisation.md) and all copy.

## What to capture
- Buying motivations (status, self-expression, care/affection for gifting, treat)
- Objections and anxieties (price justification, quality uncertainty, gift risk, returns)
- Behavioural/persuasion principles relevant to us (social proof, scarcity done tastefully, anchoring, loss aversion, reciprocity) — with ethical guardrails
- Voice-of-customer language (from reviews, support, surveys)
- **So what:** copy angles, page changes, objection-handling to test

## Key questions
- What emotional job is the purchase doing?
- What are the top 3 objections, in the customer's own words?
- Which persuasion principles fit a *premium* brand without feeling cheap?

> Evidence-based only. Behavioural principles must be applied honestly — no dark patterns (see non-negotiables in [`docs/00-north-star.md`](../../docs/00-north-star.md)).
