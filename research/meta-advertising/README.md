# Research — Meta Advertising

## Why this matters
Paid social is the primary planned acquisition channel; staying current on Meta best practice, creative patterns and benchmarks improves ROAS and reduces wasted spend. Feeds [`docs/08-meta-ads.md`](../../docs/08-meta-ads.md) and the [Meta specialist prompt](../../prompts/meta-ads.md).

## What to capture
- Account structure / campaign best practice (prospecting vs. retargeting, budget, bidding)
- Creative patterns that work for premium/gifting products (formats, hooks, angles)
- Tracking best practice (Pixel + Conversions API, signal quality, iOS/privacy impacts)
- Benchmarks (CTR, CPC, CPM, ROAS) for our category — with source & caveats
- Competitor ads (cross-link [`../competitors`](../competitors) and the Meta Ad Library)
- **So what:** test ideas for creative and structure (scored)

## Key questions
- What creative angles best sell premium scarves / gifts on Meta?
- What account structure suits our budget and catalogue?
- What ROAS/CPA benchmarks are realistic for us?

> Benchmarks are directional — our break-even ROAS (from [`docs/04-commercial-analysis.md`](../../docs/04-commercial-analysis.md)) is the real bar. Cite sources.
