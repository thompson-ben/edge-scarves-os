# Research — Fashion Trends

## Why this matters
Staying current with accessory/scarf trends — colours, materials, prints, styling — keeps the range desirable and informs marketing creative and content, without chasing fads that conflict with a premium, timeless brand. Feeds [`docs/05-product-strategy.md`](../../docs/05-product-strategy.md), [`docs/02-brand-strategy.md`](../../docs/02-brand-strategy.md) and [`docs/11-social-media.md`](../../docs/11-social-media.md).

## What to capture
- Seasonal colour/material/print trends relevant to scarves and accessories
- Styling trends and how customers wear scarves (content angles)
- What is timeless vs. faddish for our brand (filter for premium longevity)
- Trend sources (fashion press, runway, search/Pinterest trends, social) — cited
- **So what:** range/content/creative implications worth testing

## Key questions
- Which trends fit our premium, lasting positioning (vs. fast-fashion noise)?
- What styling content could drive engagement and sales?
- Are there material/colour opportunities for the next range?

> Premium filter applies: prefer enduring desirability over chasing every trend. Cite sources; separate trend fact from opinion.
