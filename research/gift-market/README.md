# Research — Gift Market

## Why this matters
Scarves are a major gifting category, and gifting buyers behave differently from self-purchasers (different motivations, urgency, objections and UX needs). Understanding them unlocks AOV, seasonal revenue and conversion. Feeds [`docs/03-customer-persona.md`](../../docs/03-customer-persona.md), [`docs/05-product-strategy.md`](../../docs/05-product-strategy.md) and [`docs/09-email-marketing.md`](../../docs/09-email-marketing.md).

## What to capture
- The gifting buyer: who they are, what they fear (wrong choice, late delivery)
- Gifting occasions & calendar (Christmas, Mother's Day, birthdays, anniversaries, Valentine's)
- Gifting UX expectations: gift wrap, gift messages, gift receipts, delivery-by dates, easy returns
- Gift-set / bundle opportunities and price points
- **So what:** gifting features and campaigns to build/test (scored)

## Key questions
- What share of our orders are gifts vs. self-purchase, and when?
- What gifting features are we missing that reduce purchase anxiety?
- Which occasions are the biggest revenue opportunities?
