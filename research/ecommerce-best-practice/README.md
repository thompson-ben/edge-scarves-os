# Research — Ecommerce Best Practice

## Why this matters
Proven DTC/Shopify patterns let us copy what works instead of rediscovering it. Covers store structure, PDP/cart/checkout design, merchandising, AOV tactics, fulfilment and retention. Feeds [`docs/06-website-audit.md`](../../docs/06-website-audit.md), [`docs/07-conversion-rate-optimisation.md`](../../docs/07-conversion-rate-optimisation.md) and [`docs/12-shopify-architecture.md`](../../docs/12-shopify-architecture.md).

## What to capture
- High-converting PDP / cart / checkout patterns (with examples)
- AOV levers (bundles, gift sets, thresholds, upsells/cross-sells)
- Trust & social-proof patterns for premium brands
- Shopify-specific tips, apps and pitfalls (speed, app bloat)
- Benchmarks (conversion, AOV, abandonment) — note source & relevance
- **So what:** patterns worth testing here, scored via [Opportunity Score](../../scorecards/opportunity-scoring-framework.md)

## Key questions
- What do the best premium Shopify stores do that we don't?
- Which AOV lever fits our range and margin?
- What benchmarks are realistic for our category/price point?

> Cite sources; adapt to *premium* — not every mass-market tactic fits the brand.
