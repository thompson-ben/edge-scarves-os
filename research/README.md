# Research

The knowledge base of **external** intelligence that informs Edge Scarves decisions — competitors, customers, markets, channels and best practice. Where [`/docs`](../docs) is what we decide, `/research` is the evidence we decide from. Good research lowers risk and raises the quality (and Opportunity Score accuracy) of every initiative.

## Structure

| Folder | Focus |
|---|---|
| [`competitors/`](competitors) | Competitor teardowns, positioning, pricing, ads. |
| [`customer-psychology/`](customer-psychology) | Why people buy — motivations, objections, behavioural triggers. |

| [`meta-advertising/`](meta-advertising) | Paid social best practice, creative patterns, benchmarks. |
| [`cro-research/`](cro-research) | Conversion principles, test ideas and case studies. |

## How to use this library

- **One topic per file:** `topic_<short-name>.md` (e.g. `competitor_brandx.md`). Use a screenshots subfolder where helpful (also see [`/screenshots`](../screenshots) and [`/assets/competitor-research`](../assets/competitor-research)).
- **Cite the source and date** for every claim. Distinguish **observed fact** from **interpretation**.
- **Never invent findings.** If something is assumed, label it; if unknown, mark `TODO`.
- **End every research note with "So what?"** — the implication for Edge Scarves and any backlog item it suggests (to be scored via the [Opportunity Score](../scorecards/opportunity-scoring-framework.md)).
- Research feeds the relevant `/docs` (e.g. competitor research → [`02-brand-strategy.md`](../docs/02-brand-strategy.md); CRO research → [`07-conversion-rate-optimisation.md`](../docs/07-conversion-rate-optimisation.md)).

> Research is input, not authorisation. It informs decisions; it does not by itself change the store.
