# Research — Competitors

## Why this matters
Knowing how competing premium scarf/accessory brands position, price, merchandise and advertise reveals gaps to exploit and standards to beat. Directly informs [`docs/02-brand-strategy.md`](../../docs/02-brand-strategy.md), [`docs/05-product-strategy.md`](../../docs/05-product-strategy.md) and [`docs/08-meta-ads.md`](../../docs/08-meta-ads.md).

## What to capture (per competitor → `competitor_<name>.md`)
- Brand, URL, positioning and price points
- Product range, hero products, materials
- Site/UX strengths & weaknesses (with screenshots)
- Ads via the **Meta Ad Library** — angles, formats, longevity
- Reviews / reputation signals
- **So what:** what to learn, what to avoid, what gap we can own

## Key questions
- Who are our 3–5 closest competitors, really?
- Where are they weak (UX, range, service, story) that we can win?
- What ad angles are working for them?

> Record only observed facts with source + date. Do not invent competitor data.
