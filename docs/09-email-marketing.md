# 09 — Email Marketing (Lifecycle & Retention)

## Purpose

Own the email (and SMS) lifecycle: the automated flows and campaigns that convert subscribers, recover lost sales, and turn one-time buyers into repeat customers. Email is the highest-margin revenue channel because it monetises an audience we already own. This document defines the flows, calendar and metrics.

## Future Vision

A complete lifecycle programme where every high-intent moment is covered by an automated flow, and a consistent campaign calendar drives repeat purchase. Email reliably contributes a meaningful share of revenue at very low cost, lifting net profit and customer lifetime value. Segmentation keeps the premium audience engaged without discount dependence.
