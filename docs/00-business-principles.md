# 00 — Business Principles

## Purpose

The operating principles that guide every Edge Scarves decision. Where the [North Star](00-north-star.md) sets the destination, these principles set *how we travel* — the consistent reasoning every specialist (human or AI) applies so decisions are coherent even when made independently. When in doubt, reason from these.

> These principles are deliberately commercial. The goal of Sprint 0.5 is to ensure future decisions are **commercially driven, not technically driven**.

## Current State

The 20 principles below are active. They are grouped for readability; the grouping is not a priority order — **Principle 1 outranks all others**.

### Commercial discipline
1. **Profit is the point.** Revenue, traffic and followers are means, not ends. We optimise for net profit.
2. **Every initiative states its £ impact before it starts.** No measurable impact, no go.
3. **Rank by leverage — £ impact ÷ effort.** Always do the highest-leverage thing available now.
4. **Prove before you scale.** Validate economics small, then scale only what works.
5. **Protect the margin.** Discounting is a tool of last resort, not a growth strategy.
6. **Spend only above break-even.** Paid acquisition must defend net profit, not just buy revenue.

### Evidence & decision-making
7. **Evidence over opinion.** Decide from data; when data is absent, make an explicit, bounded bet and label the confidence.
8. **Never invent facts.** Unknowns are `TODO`; claims are substantiated; assumptions are flagged.
9. **Verify the data before trusting it.** Bad tracking produces confident wrong decisions.
10. **Measure what you ship.** Compare actual impact to the prediction and learn from the gap.
11. **Score and rank before building.** Backlog items are ranked by the [Opportunity Score](../scorecards/opportunity-scoring-framework.md) first.

### Customer & brand
12. **The customer's job comes first.** Solve the buyer's real problem and objections, then sell.
13. **Premium is a promise we keep everywhere.** Product, copy, packaging, support and ads all reflect it.
14. **Honesty builds the brand.** Claims are true and provable; no hype, no dark patterns.
15. **Retention compounds.** Keeping a customer is cheaper than acquiring one — invest in LTV.

### Execution & craft
16. **Smallest valuable increment.** Ship thin, reversible slices; avoid big-bang risk.
17. **Reversible by default.** Prefer changes we can measure and roll back; dev theme before live.
18. **Mobile-first, fast, accessible.** Most customers are on a phone — design and test for them first.
19. **Document in the same change.** If it isn't written down, it doesn't exist. Keep the OS current.
20. **Build the system, not just the output.** Favour repeatable processes over one-off heroics.

## Future Vision

These principles become reflexive — every specialist reasons from them without being told, producing coherent, commercially-driven decisions across the business. They are revisited at retrospectives and sharpened by real experience, so the operating system gets wiser as the business grows.
