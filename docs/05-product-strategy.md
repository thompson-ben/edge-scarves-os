## Future Vision

A tight, high-margin range built around proven hero products and profitable extensions (bundles, gift sets, seasonal drops) that raise AOV and repeat purchase. New products are introduced on evidence, not whim, with a clear margin and demand thesis. The catalogue is curated to the premium persona — fewer, better, more profitable lines.
