# 02 — Brand Strategy

## Purpose

Define what Edge Scarves stands for, how it sounds and looks, and the promise it makes to customers. Brand strategy is the constraint that keeps every campaign, page and product consistent. Marketing, CRO, content and design all reference this document so the brand reads as one coherent, premium voice.

## Future Vision

Edge Scarves is instantly recognisable: a customer could identify an ad, email or product page as ours with the logo removed. The premium positioning supports healthy margins and reduces reliance on discounting. The brand becomes an asset in its own right — driving direct/branded search, repeat purchase and word of mouth.
