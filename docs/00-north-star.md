# 00 — North Star

## Purpose

The highest document in the repository. Everything else inherits from here. When any decision, sprint, prompt or document conflicts with the North Star, the North Star wins. It exists to keep every future choice — commercial, creative or technical — pointed at the same destination. Read this first; resolve disagreements here.

## Mission

**Why Edge Scarves exists (today's reason for being):**

> To make beautifully designed, genuinely premium scarves that people are proud to wear and delighted to give — and to build a disciplined business around them that earns sustainable profit.

*(Founder to confirm/refine wording — the intent above is the working statement.)*

## Vision

**Where Edge Scarves is going (the destination):**

> A recognised premium accessories brand with a repeatable, profitable growth engine — a high-converting store, disciplined acquisition, strong retention and a genuine brand customers seek out by name. Profitable at every stage, scaled only on what is proven to work.

**Commercial expression of the vision:**
- **Phase 1:** £2,000 / month **net profit** (prove the model).
- **Long term:** £10,000+ / month **net profit** (scale the proven model).

## Business Principles

The full operating principles live in [`00-business-principles.md`](00-business-principles.md). The five that matter most:

1. **Profit is the point.** Revenue without profit is vanity.
2. **Evidence over opinion.** Decide from data; label assumptions honestly.
3. **Scalability over quick fixes.** Every choice should still make sense at 10× volume.
4. **Protect the brand and the margin.** They are long-term profit.
5. **Document everything.** If it isn't written down, it doesn't exist.

## Brand Promise

**The one thing a customer can always count on:**

> A scarf that looks and feels genuinely premium, delivered with care — worth giving, worth keeping. *(Confirm final wording in [`02-brand-strategy.md`](02-brand-strategy.md).)*

We keep this promise through product quality, considered design, reliable fulfilment and honest, premium communication — never through discounting or hype.

## Definition of Success

Success is **not** traffic, followers or revenue alone. Success is:

- **Primary:** sustained **monthly net profit** at target (£2,000 → £10,000+).
- **Healthy unit economics:** positive contribution per order; paid scaled only above break-even ROAS.
- **Compounding assets:** a converting store, an owned audience (email/repeat customers), and organic/brand demand that lower blended acquisition cost over time.
- **A brand worth more each year:** rising branded search, repeat purchase and word of mouth.
- **A business that runs on its system:** decisions are documented, measured and repeatable — not dependent on heroics.

## Non-Negotiables

Lines we will not cross, regardless of short-term gain:

1. **No scaling unprofitable activity.** Spend only adds where economics work.
2. **No margin-destroying discount dependence.** Premium brands don't compete on price.
3. **No inventing facts.** Unknowns are `TODO`; claims are substantiated.
4. **No unauthorised or untested live store changes.** Dev theme first; reversible always.
5. **No dishonest or misleading marketing.** Claims must be true and provable.
6. **No customer-trust shortcuts.** Privacy, deliverability, returns and care are protected.
7. **No work without measurable business impact.** Every item ties to a KPI.

## Decision Framework

When facing any decision, apply these gates **in order**. A "no" at a higher gate stops the decision.

1. **North Star fit** — does it serve the mission/vision and respect the non-negotiables? *(If no → reject.)*
2. **Profit** — what is the expected effect on **net profit**? Is there a plausible, quantified path? *(CEO mandate, [`prompts/ceo.md`](../prompts/ceo.md).)*
3. **Evidence** — is it backed by data, or is it an explicit, bounded bet? Label the confidence.
4. **Leverage** — what is the **£ impact ÷ effort**? Prefer the highest-leverage option.
5. **Reversibility & risk** — can we measure it and roll it back? Prefer small, reversible steps.
6. **Score it** — for any feature/backlog item, run the [Opportunity Scoring Framework](../scorecards/opportunity-scoring-framework.md) and rank against everything else **before** building.

> Shorthand: **Does it fit the North Star? Does it grow profit? Is there evidence? Is it the highest-leverage, lowest-risk way? Has it been scored and ranked?** If any answer is no, fix that before proceeding.
